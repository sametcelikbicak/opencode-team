# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, and keep discussion on the work. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and publishing private information are not acceptable.

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, discussions and any space where you represent the project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, [@sametcelikbicak](https://github.com/sametcelikbicak). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, close threads or block accounts.

## Enforcement

Maintainers follow the Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines: correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban. Consequences scale with severity and repetition.
